Frequently Asked Questions

What is CoHostIQ?
CoHostIQ is a property management app designed for hospitality professionals. It helps you manage properties, track maintenance tickets, coordinate with your team, monitor reservations, and keep your operations running smoothly.
How do I get access to CoHostIQ?
CoHostIQ accounts are set up by your organization's administrator. If you need access, contact your property management company or team lead. They will provide you with login credentials.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Contact your organization's administrator or reach out to us at support@cohostiq.app and we'll help you regain access to your account.
What devices does CoHostIQ support?
CoHostIQ is available on iPhone and also supports Apple CarPlay for on-the-go access to your properties and tickets.
Does CoHostIQ use my location?
CoHostIQ may use your location to provide property-related features such as directions and nearby property lookup. Location access is optional and can be managed in your device's Settings.
How do I report a bug?
Send an email to support@cohostiq.app with a description of the issue, the steps to reproduce it, and your device model and iOS version. Screenshots are helpful.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 13, 2026

Branson Lakes Lodging ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CoHostIQ mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our service:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and role within your organization, as provided by your administrator.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app, including features used and actions taken, to improve app performance and reliability.
  • Photos: Images you capture within the app for maintenance tickets and equipment documentation. Photos are uploaded to our servers to facilitate team collaboration.
  • Location Data: If you grant permission, we may collect your location to provide property-related features. Location access is optional.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and push notification tokens to deliver notifications and ensure compatibility.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the CoHostIQ service
  • To authenticate your identity and manage access permissions
  • To send push notifications about tickets, tasks, and updates relevant to your role
  • To facilitate communication and coordination within your property management team
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app stability

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data may be shared with:

  • Your Organization: Information you submit (tickets, photos, notes) is visible to authorized members of your organization based on their role and permissions.
  • Service Providers: We may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, and push notification delivery. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), secure token-based authentication, and role-based access controls.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you wish to have your data deleted, contact your organization's administrator or email us at support@cohostiq.app. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke consent for optional data collection (e.g., location, notifications) through your device settings

Children's Privacy

CoHostIQ is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
